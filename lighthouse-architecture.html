Engine Logic

Portal submissions from offering owners, GTM leaders, practice leads, and sponsors are translated into Lighthouse records. Structured scores, evidence links, comments, readiness inputs, and dependency flags are assessed against Lighthouse criteria, adjusted by must-have rules, evidence strength, and dependencies, then converted into maturity levels and next actions.

What This Backend Model Says

Project Lighthouse is not just a scorecard. It is the backend operating model for managing offering maturity — connecting portal intake, stakeholder submissions, governed entities, relationship logic, evidence-based scoring, workflow control, and portfolio decisions, then translating those outputs into the MVP SharePoint experience, including portfolio visibility, standardised offering pages, and a structured GTM assets portal for Sales and Marketing.
