Positioning

Lighthouse is the operating model behind the portal: workbook guidance frames collection, evidence and scoring inputs feed the three core trackers, checklist and stage libraries provide maturity rules, scoring logic converts tracker data into score, level, band, progress, authority, and must-have outputs, workflow turns gaps into Action Log priorities, and the resulting portfolio data powers the MVP Sales and Marketing experience.
