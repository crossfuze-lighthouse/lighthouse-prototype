Scoring Engine
— CMI (12 criteria) · Authority Score · MIS Model · Gap logic
3 models · 2 axes · 14 ★ must-haves
›
Strength Model — CMI · 12 criteria across 3 dimensions · Scored 1–5
Strategic Alignment 50% of CMI
Business Strategy Fit 40% · Market Validation 25% · SN Alignment 20% · Competitive Position 15%
Commercial Readiness 25% of CMI
Sales Execution Capability 35% · GTM & Collateral Readiness 30% · Operational Readiness 20% · SN Commercial Partnership 15%
Delivery Expertise 25% of CMI
Technical Capability 40% · Delivery Performance 30% · Practice Currency 20% · Continuous Improvement 10%
CMI = (SA × 0.50) + (CR × 0.25) + (DE × 0.25)
Output: 1.00 – 5.00 · Scored 1–5 per criterion per session
Strength does NOT determine claimed level — Authority governs
Authority Score · 164 milestones · Evidence-gated
Formula
(SA×0.50)+(CR×0.25)+(DE×0.25) · scale 1–5 · Claimed Level = lowest dimension always
Level thresholds
L2 = 60% · L3 = 70% · L4 = 80% · L5 = 90% of milestones complete per dimension
★ hard-block
Outstanding must-have = progression blocked regardless of % score · no bypass · reviewer sign-off required
Evidence quality
Verified = counts done · Draft + named owner + date = validator discretion · Verbal / assumed = not done
Dimension split: SA 59 · CR 67 · DE 38
Total 164. Note: categories span multiple dimensions — use New Dimension column in master as authoritative source
MIS Model · Milestone Impact Score · Drives Asset Roadmap quarters
Base points
★ must-have = 4 pts · Non-★ = 1 pt
Dimension multiplier
SA = ×2.0 · CR = ×1.0 · DE = ×1.0 · Source: New Dimension column in Scoring_Master.xlsx
Override bonus
+2 pts · manual · assessor-applied · blocked offerings only · YES/NO column in MIS Model tab
MIS = (Base pts × Dim multiplier) + Override
Q1: 8–10 · Q2: 4–7 · Q3: 2–3 · Q4: 0–1
Gap logic
High CMI + Low Authority = over-claiming. High CMI + High Authority = externally defensible. "Strength is the claim · Authority is the receipt."

Maturity Model
— L1–L5 definitions · GTM pyramid · progression rules · thresholds
5 levels · 4 stages
›
Level definitions · applies to both GTM and Partner Framework
L1 — Ad Hoc 0%
No repeatable process · activity driven by individuals not systems · no external claims
L2 — Developing 60%
Basic processes exist · inconsistently applied · foundational assets in place
L3 — Standardized 70%
Consistent process · externally defensible claims · minimum bar for customer-facing work
L4 — Advanced 80%
Core differentiators active · data-driven · ready to compete on specialisation
L5 — Best-In-Class 90%
1–2 owned market positions · SN-recognised · industry-validated
GTM pyramid — Union Square Consulting
Fundamentals · L1 → L2
Basic assets · ICP · pricing model · methodology. Never skip — foundation must exist before moving up.
Adoption · L2 → L3
Repeatable sales motion · external case studies · consistent delivery. Minimum bar for any GTM activity.
Optimization · L3 → L4
Win rate tracked · SN alignment formalised · demand gen with data. Core differentiators unlock here.
Acceleration · L4 → L5
1–2 owned market positions · co-sell agreement · NRR engine. Best-in-class standing.
Progression rules — non-negotiable
Never accelerate from L1–L2
Fundamentals must be solid. No shortcuts to L3.
L3 = minimum bar for external claims
Below L3 = internal only. No customer-facing claims on this offering.
L4 = core differentiators
Win rate, SN overlay, demand gen data all required. Active competitive differentiator.
L5 = 1–2 positions max
Only 1–2 offerings target L5 at any time. NPS, SN recognition, market ownership required.
★ blocks any level gate
A single outstanding ★ must-have prevents progression regardless of milestone % score.

Assessment Process
— Workflow · role sections · approval chain · trigger sequence
7 roles · 4 steps · quarterly
›
Workflow steps — per offering per quarter
Step 1 — Offering Owner kicks off
Selects offering · confirms quarter · opens session for assessors
Step 2 — Each assessor completes their section
Sees only their assigned categories · ticks milestones · selects evidence type (DOC/DATA/REF/LINK/CERT/SCREEN) · scores Strength 1–5 · submits (locks inputs)
Step 3 — Reviewer approves ★ must-haves
Reviews all 14 ★ must-haves from submitted sections · Verified evidence required · no drafts for ★ · Approve ✓ or Return ✗ per item · all must be approved to proceed
Step 4 — Final approval fires trigger chain
MIS recalculates · Action Log updates · Asset Roadmap quarters shift · Authority scores update · Claimed level re-evaluates · is_blocked flag updates
